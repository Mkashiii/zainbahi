The Problem

Saudi SMEs are drowning in compliance complexity

With ZATCA's accelerated reform agenda, VAT at 15%, dual-regime Zakat + CIT, and mandatory e-invoicing — the compliance burden on Saudi SMEs has never been heavier.

⏳

40–80 hours per filing cycle spent manually mapping GL accounts, computing Zakat bases, and preparing VAT returns — often by expensive consultants.

⚠️

Mixed ownership is a compliance minefield. Most SME finance teams don't correctly bifurcate Zakat (Saudi) vs. Corporate Tax (foreign) — leading to ZATCA penalties.

📋

No single platform handles Zakat + CIT + VAT + FATOORAH + risk scoring together. SMEs juggle 3–5 consultants, spreadsheets, and manual portals.

AI Account Classification
LLM-powered mapping of any trial balance to ZATCA chart of accounts — with confidence scores and human-in-the-loop review.

  • One-click approve/reject workflow
  • Custom account mapping library
